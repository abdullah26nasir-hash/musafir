# Musafir - Brand Kit v1

## The world (from Brand Kata 001)
**Contradiction (the premise):** The most-travelled sacred journey on earth - and pilgrims face real, unglamorous danger (crushing crowds, 45°C heat, targeted scams) that nobody designs for. Musafir treats pilgrim safety as sacred, not as a disclaimer in a footer.

**Story enough people believe:** "If Musafir says it's fine, it's fine." The app earns belief by showing its sources (FCDO, Nusuk, live data), never hiding a hard truth, and never being wrong about safety.

**Archetypes we design for:** the first-time pilgrim (anxious, needs hand-holding), the family organiser (booking for parents, needs trust), the repeat mutamir (wants efficiency, respects depth).

**Language:** mutamir, tawaf, sa'i, miqat, Nusuk, Haram - used correctly and respectfully, never translated away, never gamified. No streaks, no points, no confetti. This is not a game.

**External credibility is associative:** FCDO advisories, official permit links, real hotels, real price bands. The app borrows belief from real institutions, visibly, on every screen.

**Tone of voice (Kata 003):** open on common ground ("Crowds are the real risk"), frame-shift to our world ("here's what the official advice says right now"), low decoding load (short sentences, plain words, one idea per card), stories over stats where possible.

## Look
- **Feel:** light, calm, unhurried. Morning light, not nightclub. Generous whitespace. Safety information carries gravity, not alarm.
- **Palette:**
  - `paper` #F7F4EC - warm ivory background
  - `surface` #FFFFFF - cards
  - `ink` #1B2A22 - deep green-black text
  - `dome` #156A52 - primary brand green (Prophet's Mosque dome)
  - `gold` #A8842F - accent only: the mark, key numbers, focus rings
  - `mist` #66736A - secondary text
  - status: `clear` #2E7D4F, `caution` #B07A1E, `warning` #B3382C
- **Type:** display = Fraunces (warm, editorial serif - gravity without stiffness), body = Manrope, Arabic = Amiri. Tabular numerals for times/prices.
- **The mark:** eight-point star (khatam) in dome green on ivory, with a small gold centre point. Used at 20px (header) to 560px (welcome watermark). Never on busy backgrounds.
- **Signature element:** the Readiness triad - three calm cards (Advice / Heat / Crowds) that summarise "is it fine right now" in one glance. It appears on the welcome screen and again in the trip view.
- **Imagery:** no stock photos of the Kaaba (rights + cliche). Abstract geometric patterns (girih) at very low opacity only.

## Rules
- Never say "safe" without a source and a timestamp next to it.
- Advisory states use three levels only: CLEAR / CAUTION / WARNING, with the official wording one tap away.
- Light theme only. Dark mode is not part of this brand.
- Mobile-first; every screen must work one-handed on a 360px phone.
